Septic Arthritis & Posterior DislocationEmergency
Septic arthritis — synovial fluid (Margaretten 2007 JAMA) M: WBC > 25,000/µL: LR 2.9 (2.5–3.4); > 50,000/µL: LR 7.7 (5.7–11.0)
Posterior dislocation (Xu 2015 J Orthop Translat) L: rare (< 4% of all shoulder dislocations); initial diagnosis missed in 150 patients (73.2%); AP radiograph alone gave the right diagnosis in only 19 of 166 (11.4%); axillary / Y view / CT confirmed all 205
Traumatic Anterior Dislocation Associated InjuriesBrownson 2015
Associated injury rates (Brownson 2015 BESS/BOA) (Guideline): clinically apparent neurological injury ≈ 13.5% of dislocations (axillary nerve two-thirds); EMG shows nerve injury in 48%; greater-tuberosity fracture ≈ 16%; rotator cuff tears 41% (40–55 y), 71% (56–70 y), 100% (> 70 y)
Nerve injury follow-up (Brownson 2015) (Guideline): “EMG studies should be performed at 6 weeks if no clinical recovery is seen” → nerve-injury centre referral if no recovery
