Park clusters (Park 2005 JBJS Am) M: HK + painful arc + infraspinatus test → 95% post-test probability of any impingement; painful arc + drop-arm + infraspinatus test → 91% for full-thickness tear

Drop-arm test (Park 2005) M & Hornblower sign E: posterosuperior cuff integrity screen
ER lag sign (ERLS): Sp 97% for subacromial pain (injection-confirmed), highest of all tests (Cadogan 2016) M; ERLS less sensitive but more specific than Jobe; lag size correlates with tear size (Hertel 1996) L
IR lag sign (IRLS) (Hertel 1996) L: IRLS more sensitive than lift-off; lag size correlates with tear size
Murrell cuff-tear cluster (Murrell & Walton 2001 Lancet) L: supraspinatus weakness + ER weakness + impingement sign all positive (or 2 positive and age ≥ 60) → 98% chance of cuff tear
Bear-hug (Barth 2006 Arthroscopy) L: Sn 60%, Sp 91.7%

Lift-off test (Barth 2006) L: Sn 17.6%, Sp 100%
