3 · Bankart vs Latarjet & Return to Sport HOSSEIN ZADEH · MEMON
Bankart vs Latarjet (MA, 21 studies, 13,176 shoulders) (Hossein Zadeh 2024 J Shoulder Elbow Surg) M: arthroscopic Bankart RR 3.08 (2.03–4.68) for recurrence/revision vs Latarjet; Rowe +4.55 for Latarjet; total complications ≈ 47% lower with Bankart (RR 0.53, 0.31–0.90)
Return to sport after arthroscopic Bankart (SR, 34 studies) (Memon 2018 J Shoulder Elbow Surg) M: any sport 81% (74–87%); pre-injury level 66% (57–74%); ≈ one-third do not return to pre-injury level

4 · Subacromial Decompression & MID / PASS CSAW · FIMPACT · KANTO
CSAW (n = 313) (Beard 2018 Lancet) H: OSS at 6 months: decompression 32.7 vs arthroscopy only 34.2, MD −1.3 (−3.9 to 1.3); both surgical groups only a small benefit over no treatment (2.8 points vs decompression)
FIMPACT (24 months) (Paavola 2018 BMJ) H: ASD vs diagnostic arthroscopy: pain VAS −4.6 (−11.3 to 2.1) at rest, −9.0 (−18.1 to 0.2) on activity — no clinically relevant difference
Decompression vs exercise — 12 years (Ketola 2017 Bone Joint J) M: no significant difference in pain between surgery and exercise
MID / PASS after subacromial surgery (Kanto 2021 BMC Med Res Methodol) M: MID: pain on activity 20 mm, Constant 10 points, SST 1.5; PASS: Constant 80, SST 11
5 · Frozen Shoulder Surgical Trials (UK FROST & Kivimäki) RANGAN 2020 LANCET
Frozen shoulder — UK FROST (n = 503) (Rangan 2020 Lancet) H: 12-month OSS: capsular release 40.3, MUA 38.3, early structured physio 37.2; all differences below target → none clinically superior; capsular release 8 serious adverse events, MUA 2; MUA most cost-effective
MUA + home exercise vs home exercise (Kivimäki 2007 J Shoulder Elbow Surg) M: no difference in pain or working ability at any time; small ROM gains with MUA
