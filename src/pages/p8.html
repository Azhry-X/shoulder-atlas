1 · Rotator Cuff / RCRSP Exercise Trials & Dosing Principles GRASP · HOLMGREN · SELF · INGWERSEN · SMITH · SAITO
GRASP (n = 708) (Hopewell 2021 Lancet) H: progressive exercise vs one best-practice advice session: SPADI over 12 months −0.66 (99% CI −4.52 to 3.20) — no difference; injection vs no injection −1.11 (−4.47 to 2.26)
Specific exercise (Holmgren) (Holmgren 2012 BMJ) H: Constant-Murley +24 vs +9 points (MD 15, 8.5–20.6); surgery chosen by 20% vs 63% (OR 7.7, 3.1–19.4)
Single self-managed exercise (SELF) (Littlewood 2016 Clin Rehabil) M: SPADI at 3 months 32.4 vs 30.7 usual physio; adjusted MD 3.2 (−6.0 to 12.4) — comparable
Heavy vs low load (RCT, n = 100) (Ingwersen 2017 Orthop J Sports Med) M: DASH adjusted difference −1.37 (−6.72 to 3.99) — no superiority of high-load
Painful vs pain-free exercise (MA) (Smith 2017 BJSM) M: short-term pain SMD −0.27 (−0.54 to −0.05) in favour of painful exercise; no difference later
Scapular-focused exercise (MA, 6 RCTs) (Saito 2018 Physiother Theory Pract) M: short-term pain MD −0.88 (−1.19 to −0.58), function −11.31 (−17.20 to −5.41); no difference at follow-up
2 · Full-Thickness Tears: Exercise vs Repair KUKKONEN · MOOSMAYER
Non-traumatic cuff tears > 55 y (RCT, 180 shoulders) (Kukkonen 2014 Bone Joint J) H: Constant at 1 year: physio 74.1, acromioplasty 77.2, repair 77.9 (p = 0.34)
Small/medium tears — 10 years (Moosmayer 2019 JBJS Am) H: repair better than physio by 9.6 Constant points (p = 0.002), ASES 15.7; crossovers to late surgery 10.0 points worse than primary repair

MDI: Watson vs Rockwood programme (Warby 2018 Am J Sports Med) M: WOSI effect 11.1 (1.9–20.2) at 12 weeks; 12.6 (3.4–21.9) at 24 weeks — favours Watson
Frozen shoulder education Kelley 2013 (Guideline): JOSPT CPG: educate on natural course, activity modification, and match stretching intensity to irritability
4 · Overhead Throwers — GIRD, Total Rotational Motion (TRM) & External Rotation WILK 2011 · WILK 2015

Throwers — ER (Wilk 2015 Am J Sports Med) L: insufficient ER (< 5° more ER on throwing side): 2.2× disabled-list risk (1.2–4.1), 4.0× surgery (1.5–12.6); GIRD not significant
