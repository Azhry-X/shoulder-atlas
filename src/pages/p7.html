1 · Rotator Cuff Manual Therapy PAGE 2016 · DESJARDINS-CHARBONNEAU
MT + exercise for cuff disease (Cochrane, 60 trials) (Page 2016 Cochrane) M: vs placebo at 22 weeks: pain MD 6.8 (−0.70 to 14.30), function MD 7.1 (0.30 to 13.90) on 100-point scales — may not be clinically important
MT for cuff tendinopathy (MA) (Desjardins-Charbonneau 2015 JOSPT) L: pain MD 1.1 (0.6–1.6) on 10-cm VAS vs placebo/added; MT added to exercise: 1.0 (0.7–1.4); effect on function unclear
2 · Frozen Shoulder Mobilisation vs Supervised Neglect PAGE 2014 · VERMEULEN · DIERCKS
MT + exercise for frozen shoulder (Cochrane) (Page 2014 Cochrane) M: at 7 weeks probably less improvement than glucocorticoid injection (pain MD 26 points, 15–37, on 100)
High-grade vs low-grade mobilisation (Vermeulen 2006 Phys Ther) M: HGMT more effective for mobility and disability; overall differences small
Supervised neglect vs intensive stretching (Diercks 2004 J Shoulder Elbow Surg) L: Constant ≥ 80 at 24 months: 89% supervised neglect vs 63% intensive; 64% within 12 months
3 · Thoracic SMT & Cervicothoracic CPR KARDOUNI · PEEK · MINTKEN
Thoracic SMT vs sham (SIS) (Kardouni 2015 JOSPT) M: both groups improved (NPRS 1.2, Penn 9.1) with no difference between real and sham
Thoracic manual therapy (SR) (Peek 2015 J Man Manip Ther) L: 3 RCTs: reduced pain and disability vs usual care up to 52 weeks
Cervicothoracic manipulation CPR (Mintken 2010 Phys Ther) L: ≥ 3 of 5 variables: success 61% → 89% (LR+ 5.3); unvalidated single-arm study
4 · Dry Needling, Kinesio Taping & Stretching NAVARRO-SANTANA · CELIK · MCCLURE
Dry needling (MA) (Navarro-Santana 2021 Phys Ther) M: pain MD −0.49 (−0.84 to −0.13) — small, short term; disability MD −9.99 (−15.97 to −4.01) — low quality
Kinesio taping (MA, 14 studies) (Celik 2020 Clin Rehabil) M: no better than sham for pain (MD −0.77, −1.77 to 0.22) or function → no firm evidence of benefit
Posterior shoulder tightness (McClure 2007 JOSPT) L: cross-body stretch +20.0° IR (SD 12.9) vs control +5.9° (P = .009); sleeper stretch +12.4° (not significant)
